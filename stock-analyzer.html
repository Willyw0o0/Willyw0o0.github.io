Does the rule beat just holding?

A backtesting project that tests rule-based trading strategies against historical price data, then checks whether they actually beat buying and doing nothing.

the idea

Most people assume active trading beats holding. This project doesn't assume — it tests. A strategy is a fixed, repeatable rule for buying and selling. The backtester replays that rule against years of real price data, day by day, then compares the outcome to a simple buy-and-hold baseline on the same stock and period.

strategies

  • Moving average crossover — buys when a short-term average price crosses above a long-term average, sells when it crosses below.
  • RSI, MACD, and Bollinger Bands planned as the project expands.

stocks

Daily OHLCV data, aligned to a shared window from July 2010 through March 2020.

AAPL — stable large‑cap
TSLA — volatile growth
INTC — semiconductor
MSFT — stable large‑cap

metrics

Each strategy is scored on total return, max drawdown, win rate, and Sharpe ratio — then measured against what buy-and-hold would have returned over the same stretch.
